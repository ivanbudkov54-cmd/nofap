Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the NoFap app (“the App”) collects, uses, and deletes information. The App is designed so that you can use it without creating an email account.

Information we collect

  • Anonymous user ID. If cloud sync is enabled, the App creates an anonymous account and stores a random user identifier. We do not ask for your name, email address, or phone number for this account.
  • Streak data. The App stores the start time of your current streak, the number of days, your best streak, and the time of a relapse if you record one.
  • Mood notes stored for your account. Diary entries you choose to save, including a mood score, an urge score, an optional prompt, and the note text, are stored with your anonymous user ID so they can be shown back to you.

Content-blocking choices and onboarding answers stay on your device unless a feature you turn on explicitly sends them.

How we use information

We use this information only to operate the App: to show your streak, restore your diary, and delete your data when you ask. We do not sell personal information. We do not share it with advertisers, and we do not use it for cross-app advertising or tracking.

How information is protected

Data sent to our backend is transmitted over HTTPS and stored in a database that only your anonymous account can read or change. The sign-in token is kept in the iOS Keychain on your device. No method of storage is perfectly secure, but we limit access to what the App needs to function.

Data retention

We keep streak data and diary notes for as long as your anonymous account exists. If you delete the App without using Delete Account, a copy may remain on the server until you ask us to remove it.

Data deletion

You can delete your information in either of these ways:

  • In the App. Open Settings and tap “Delete Account and Data”. This permanently deletes your profile, streak history, relapse records, and diary notes, signs you out, and returns the App to a new guest session.
  • By request. Email support@example.com and ask us to delete your data. Include any identifier the App has shown you, if you have one. We will delete the account data we can match to that request.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
