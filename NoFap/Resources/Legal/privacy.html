Last updated: October 4, 2026 This Privacy Policy describes how the Spartan Discipline & Streak Tracker application (“the App”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, and safeguards your information. 1. Information We Collect We adhere to data minimization principles. We do not require your real name, phone number, or contacts to use the App. - Account Identifiers: When cloud synchronization is enabled, the App generates a cryptographically secure random user identifier (User UUID) and a unique 6-character profile invite code. If you register via email, your email address is securely stored for authentication purposes only. - Habit and Streak Metrics: The App stores your streak counters, milestone achievements, daily check-in timestamps, target duration selections (7, 21, 45, or 90 days), and relapse timestamps if recorded. - Journal and Mood Logs: Self-reflection notes, urge ratings, and mood check-ins you choose to submit are stored encrypted alongside your User UUID solely for your personal review. - Social and Accountability Features: - Buddy & Squad Pairing: When you sync with a buddy or squad via code, we store the relationship link between the respective User UUIDs. - In-App Chat: Messages exchanged within the 1-on-1 buddy chat are processed and stored via secure real-time backend channels solely to deliver conversations to the intended participants. - SOS Notifications: If you trigger the SOS alert feature, a device notification token (APNs token) is used to dispatch an urgent push alert to your designated buddy. - Purchase Data: All financial transactions are processed directly by Apple via the App Store. We do not store or have access to your credit card or financial details. We collect anonymous receipt identifiers via RevenueCat to verify active Pro access. 2. How We Use Information We process your data strictly to provide and maintain the App's core functionality: - Tracking and displaying your recovery streaks and avatar rank progression. - Facilitating synchronized accountability with your chosen buddy and squad. - Delivering real-time chat messages and SOS emergency push notifications. - Validating subscription entitlement status. We do NOT sell personal data. We do NOT use third-party advertising SDKs, cross-app tracking, or data brokers. 3. Data Storage, Security, and Retention - Transmission and Encryption: All network communication occurs strictly over HTTPS/TLS encryption. Data is stored on secure cloud database servers with strict Row-Level Security (RLS) policies ensuring users can only read and write their own authorized records. - Authentication tokens are preserved within the secure iOS Keychain. - Retention: Data is retained for the lifetime of your active profile. Inactive guest profiles without recorded activity for 12 months may be automatically purged. 4. Account and Data Deletion (App Store Guideline 5.1.1(v)) You maintain absolute control over your records. You may permanently delete your data through either method: - In-App Self-Service: Navigate to Settings → tap "Delete Account and Data". This immediately and permanently purges your profile, streak records, journal logs, buddy connections, and chat history from our servers. - Email Request: Send an email to support@spartanapp.com with your profile invite code. We will execute complete deletion within 30 days. 5. Children’s Privacy The App is strictly not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. 6. Changes to This Policy We may update this policy periodically. Revisions will be published on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. 7.
